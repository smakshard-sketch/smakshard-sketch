Install in 4 steps

  1. Tap Download for Android, then download the .apk file of the latest version.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser (“Unknown sources”).
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Follow the short 3-screen guide. You are ready.

To update, simply install the new version over the old one: your data is kept.

Tutorials

1. Add a deadline
  1. Tap the Add button.
  2. Write a sentence in the top box, for example “Rent 150 USD on the 5th”, or fill in the form: title, amount, currency, date.
  3. Choose your reminders (3 days before, on the day…), then Save.
2. Paste a Mobile Money SMS
  1. Copy the confirmation SMS (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Orange Money…).
  2. Tap Add and paste the SMS in the top box.
  3. Amount, date and reference are filled in, and the operation is already marked as paid. Check it, then save.
3. Get your reminders
  1. At first launch, accept notifications.
  2. If reminders do not arrive: Android Settings → Apps → ÉCHÉANCE → Notifications, and allow them. On some phones, also turn off battery optimization for ÉCHÉANCE.
4. Create an account and use 2 phones
  1. In the app: Settings → My account → create an account with your email.
  2. Confirm the email you receive, then choose a backup password and write it down: it protects your data and we cannot recover it.
  3. On the 2nd phone, install the app and sign in with the same account. Your deadlines arrive by themselves.
  4. If the bell warns you about an unknown sign-in, tap It's not me: an email lets you change your password.
5. Go PRO (M-Pesa payment)
  1. Tap the TRIAL or PRO button at the top of the home screen.
  2. Choose an offer: 1 month $1.99, 3 months $4.99 or 1 year $17.99.
  3. Send the amount by M-Pesa, then enter the transaction reference in the app.
  4. After checking, your PRO code arrives in the app. It works on 2 phones at most.
